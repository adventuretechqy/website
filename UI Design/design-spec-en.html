06 · Motion

Motion System

Unified and restrained: everything completes within 0.15–0.3s, built on three feedback patterns — lift + shadow + color flip. No flashy animation. Hover the swatches below.

Card lift

Product card lifts 6px, news card 5px, pill 3px — higher hierarchy lifts more

Shadow deepen

No shadow by default → large shadow on hover, e.g. product card 0 20px 48px rgba(0,0,0,.10)

Border flips blue

On hover the border goes from #E8E8E8 to brand blue — a unified signal of interactivity

Button color flip

Outline buttons swap foreground/background on hover; primary buttons darken; press scale(0.98)

Underline grow

On hover the nav link's blue underline expands via scaleX from the left (0→1, 0.2s)

Image micro-zoom

Inner image scale(1.05) on card hover, 0.3s — consistently zooming upward

07 · UX Decisions

UX Decisions (design intent read from the code)

1
Sticky nav + glass

95% white + backdrop-filter blur(12px); content shows through while scrolling without hurting readability

2
Scroll anchor nav

scroll-behavior: smooth; nav links straight to section ids for jump-free single-page browsing

3
JS-free tabs

Pure CSS radio hack for tabs, zero dependencies, zero flicker — horizontal scroll on mobile

4
Progressive hero

Hero tries AVIF → WebP → MP4 video in order, while respecting prefers-reduced-motion

5
Accessibility details

Decorative SVGs marked aria-hidden, interactive controls carry aria-label, images have full alt

6
Performance first

All images loading="lazy" (except above-the-fold); hamburger collapses nav on mobile

7
Persistent conversion entry

Floating WhatsApp / email / back-to-top at bottom-right, always within reach of sales — B2B inquiry-oriented

8
Clear action hierarchy

One primary + one secondary button per section; whole cards clickable (product/news) to enlarge hit areas

This system in one sentence

"Sharp-cornered buttons × soft rounded cards × a single brand blue" — build hierarchy with large type + heavy weights, use blue only to mark "interactive", and keep the whole site feeling consistent through unified hover feedback (lift / border-blue / color-flip). A mature style well suited to B2B premium hardware: professional, restrained, trustworthy.
