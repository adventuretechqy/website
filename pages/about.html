Profile

AdventureTech: Expert in Ceramic 3D Printing

AdventureTech is a cutting-edge technology company specializing in the development of ceramic 3D printing technology, equipment, and materials. Our core team comprises highly qualified professionals from prestigious institutions such as Tsinghua University and the Norwegian University of Science and Technology — including professors, postdoctoral researchers, and PhDs, who form the backbone of our company's core competitiveness.

Our Vision

Our vision is to promote national self-reliance through innovation, create value for the country, and fulfill our social responsibilities. Guided by the principles of technological innovation and integrity, AdventureTech is committed to delivering comprehensive and integrated ceramic 3D printing solutions to our customers.

Development

Development History

Milestones on our journey from a startup founded in 2015 to a complete ceramic 3D printing solutions provider.

2015

Shenzhen Adventure Tech Co., Ltd. was established

Founded as a high-tech company focused on ceramic additive manufacturing technology. Our R&D team consists primarily of postdoctoral researchers from Tsinghua University and returning PhDs from the Norwegian University of Science and Technology, dedicated to complete "ceramic 3D printing equipment + materials + sintering process" solutions.

2018

Standard Edition Precision Direct-Write 3D Printer

Successfully developed the Adventure-3D-LB-Printer standard edition, capable of printing paste-like ink materials to achieve high precision and quality. Our proprietary ADT-Slicer 4D printing engine enables one-stroke forming and integrates the CAD-4D printing engine for direct conversion of CAD paths into motion codes.

2020

Experimental-Level DLP Ceramic 3D Printer

Successfully developed an experimental-level DLP photopolymerization ceramic 3D printer with multi-material compatibility for high-precision printing of ceramic photopolymer materials. Its dual-cylinder-sinking-scraping structure requires only 80 mL of ceramic material to start printing.

2021

Industrial-Grade Ultra-High-Speed DLP Ceramic 3D Printer

Successfully developed a DLP peristaltic ceramic 3D printer with a peristaltic feeding structure that accommodates high-viscosity, high-solid-content ceramic slurries with automatic filtration. Printing speed reaches up to 600 layers per hour, replacing traditional injection and molding processes.

2023

DLP Desktop Peristaltic Ceramic 3D Printer

Developed the desktop ceramic printing device ADT-3D-ZM-Ceramic-Printer for laboratory use. Using upright DLP projection technology, it achieves up to 700 layers per hour with a dual-scraper structure and a peristaltic-pump filtration system, widely applicable in medical, aerospace, and environmental protection fields.
