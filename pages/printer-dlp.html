DLP Ceramic 3D Printer.

Ultra-fast, high-precision, large-format ceramic 3D printing — from research to production.

Equipment Products

Ultra-fast, high-precision, large-format.

Adventure Technology DLP ceramic 3D printers use advanced Digital Light Processing to deliver dense, high-strength ceramic parts with fine detail and smooth surfaces. Four configurations cover every stage — from lab R&D to industrial production.

Request a Quote View Specs

Why DLP

Engineered for Performance

Ultra-fast

Up to 700 layers per hour (alumina), accelerating both R&D iteration and production throughput.

High Success Rate

Upright sinking forming method plus a peristaltic filter feeding system improves yield and consistency.

Multi-material

Validated for oxides, non-oxides, piezoelectric ceramics, bioceramics and photosensitive resins.

One-click Printing

Built-in material process parameter packages and software correction tools simplify operation.
