Formnext 2025: Face-To-Face with ADT Ceramic 3D Printing

Enterprise News 2025-11-24 By ADT ceramic 3D printing

Introduction to Formnext 2025

From November 18th to 21st, Formnext 2025, the annual global event for the additive manufacturing and 3D printing industry, grandly opened in Frankfurt! Since its first staging at the Frankfurt International Exhibition Center in 2015, Formnext has won widespread acclaim from the industry for its exceptional scale, abundant exhibitor resources, and professional audience. After years of accumulation and development, Formnext has achieved leapfrog growth in scale, exhibitors, and audience numbers, making it the world's leading industry exhibition.

Formnext 2025 Event Overview

The event brought together over 800 exhibitors from multiple countries, reaching a new high in international participation. The exhibition hall was bustling with activity, and industry elites from all over the world exchanged ideas and collaborated, jointly opening a new chapter in additive manufacturing. As an innovative practitioner in the field of ceramic additive manufacturing, Adventure Technology (ADT) also made its appearance at the event, engaging in in-depth discussions with German visitors about the revolution in ceramic 3D printing and exploring industry cooperation and development.

Adventure Technology (ADT)'s outstanding performance at Formnext 2025

At Formnext 2025, Adventure Technology (ADT) showcased its desktop DLP ceramic 3D printing equipment and a series of samples, fully demonstrating its technical capabilities in the field of ceramic additive manufacturing. Adventure Technology (ADT)'s DLP ceramic 3D printing equipment boasts four core advantages: high speed (up to approximately 1400 cm³/h), high precision (up to 25μm optical precision), high stability (successful sample rate as high as 99%), and strong material compatibility (up to 100 Pa·s). Whether for scientific research or large-scale production, Adventure Technology (ADT) provides users with reliable ceramic 3D printing solutions through a stable, professional, and verifiable equipment system. At the exhibition, Adventure Technology (ADT)'s advanced desktop ceramic 3D printing equipment and high-precision printed samples attracted the interest of international professional visitors, leading to lively discussions. Even the CEO of international 3D industry giant 3D Cream expressed his admiration for Adventure Technology (ADT)'s technology. At Formnext 2025 in Germany, Adventure Technology (ADT) showcased a comprehensive matrix of ceramic samples covering multiple material systems, high-precision structures, and applications ranging from research-grade to industrial-scale mass production:

Structural Ceramics: Representative structural components of key materials such as alumina, zirconia, silicon nitride, and silicon carbide, demonstrating high density, high strength, and complex geometry capabilities;

Functional Ceramics: Various systems including dielectric, transparent, bioactive, and piezoelectric ceramics, showcasing Adventure Technology (ADT)'s in-depth R&D capabilities in materials;

Ultra-fine Samples: Sub-millimeter fine pillar arrays, thin-walled structures, micro-channel structures, and complex topological lattice components, allowing on-site visitors to intuitively see the extreme molding precision and stability of the equipment;

Research-grade Custom Parts: Highly complex samples used for new material verification, microstructure design, and gradient structure research, reflecting our ability to jointly promote material innovation with global research users;

Industrial-grade Mass Production Products: Mass-produced wear-resistant parts, dental zirconia, precision fixtures, and small precision structural parts, demonstrating Adventure Technology (ADT)'s full-process capabilities from "successful printing" to "stable mass production".

Behind these exhibits are Adventure Technology (ADT)'s continuous breakthroughs in core areas such as ceramic slurry systems, photopolymerization molding, high-speed printing, precision control, sintering processes, and mass production quality management. This presented international professional visitors with a complete, mature, and practical industrial system for ceramic 3D printing. From material exploration at the research end to large-scale mass production at the industrial end – Adventure Technology (ADT) is ready to meet the challenges of global users.

Formnext 2025 Ceramic 3D Printing Industry Exhibition successfully concludes

The Formnext 2025 exhibition in Frankfurt was held from November 18th to November 21st and has now successfully concluded. Adventure Technology (ADT) looks forward to meeting you again soon, where we can comprehensively showcase our complete ceramic 3D printing solutions, discuss material development, process optimization, and application implementation of ceramic 3D printing face-to-face, and jointly explore more possibilities of additive manufacturing!
