Ceramic 3D Printing Materials

Oxide, non-oxide, bioceramics and photosensitive resin for photopolymerization 3D printing.

Materials

Oxide Ceramics Non-oxide Ceramics Bioceramics Photosensitive Resin

Oxide Ceramics

Key Features

  • High-temperature & corrosion resistance: Alumina and zirconia for high-performance components.
  • High precision: Suitable for photopolymerization 3D printing of complex geometries.
  • Proven slurry models: ADT-Al2O3 and ADT-ZrO2 series with relative density up to 99.2%.
View Details

Non-oxide Ceramics

Key Features

  • Wear resistance & thermal conductivity: Silicon carbide and silicon nitride for extreme environments.
  • Chemical stability: Stable under demanding conditions.
  • Good fluidity: Enables fabrication of complex heterogeneous structures via photopolymerization.
View Details

Bioceramics

Key Features

  • Excellent biocompatibility: HAP and TCP for medical and biomedical engineering.
  • High precision: Ideal for dental and bone repair applications.
  • Good mechanical properties: Suitable for patient-specific implants and scaffolds.
View Details

Photosensitive Resin

Key Features

  • Designed for ceramic 3D printing: Formulated with ceramic powders for photopolymerization.
  • High precision & molding performance: Good fluidity and stability.
  • Controlled particle distribution: Suitable for alumina, zirconia, and silicon carbide.
View Details
