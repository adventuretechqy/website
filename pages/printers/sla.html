SLA Ceramic 3D Printer

Laser beam forming builds robust ceramic parts point-by-point with exceptional surface finish and dimensional stability.

SLA Ceramic 3D Printer

Key Features

  • The laser beam gradually forms robust ceramic prototypes or finished products by creating points into lines and lines into surfaces.
  • Provides exceptional surface finish and dimensional stability.
  • Suitable for high-end manufacturing, medical devices, art creation, and research experiments.
