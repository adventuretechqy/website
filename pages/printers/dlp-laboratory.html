DLP Ceramic 3D Printer For Laboratory

Optimized for high-viscosity ceramic materials with a precision scraper and dual-cylinder feeding for detailed research projects.

DLP Ceramic 3D Printer For Laboratory

Key Features

  • High viscosity compatibility: Precision scraper ensures smooth leveling, accommodating high-viscosity ceramic materials.
  • Material savings: Dual-cylinder hopper structure requires only 80mL to start printing.
  • High optical precision: 1080P UV light source, with optical distortion ≤0.1%, achieving optical precision up to 25μm.
  • High printing success rate: Upright sinking forming method effectively prevents "pulling," ensuring a higher success rate.
